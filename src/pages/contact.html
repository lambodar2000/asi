    Frequently asked questions

    My child has just been diagnosed. Where do I start?

    Take a breath — you are not alone. Email autismsocietyofindia2006@gmail.com and a parent volunteer will reach out. They can guide you through the first steps: confirming the diagnosis, finding therapy and schools near you, applying for the UDID card, and joining a parent support circle.

    You can also become a member to join our parent groups, and read our guide to early steps.

    Does ASI provide therapy directly?

    ASI is a parent-run advocacy and resource society — we don’t run a clinic. We connect you with therapy centres, schools and professionals through our resources directory, and support you through workshops and peer groups.

    How do I donate?

    Visit our donation page for bank transfer details. ASI is a registered society, and you’ll receive a receipt within 7 business days.

    I’m a professional or therapist. How can I get involved?

    We welcome professionals as members (200+ already are), as volunteer speakers at parent workshops, and as contributors to our resources directory. Email us with your background and how you’d like to help.

    When will the helpline be live?

    A dedicated helpline number is in the works and will be published here as soon as it’s active. Until then, please email us — our parent volunteers will respond promptly.
