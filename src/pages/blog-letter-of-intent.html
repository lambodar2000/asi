Driving impact beyond the agenda: Workshop on Letter of Intent & Private Trust

Workshop overview

The session focused on “Letter of Intent & Private Trust for your Special Need Child” — a critical but often under-addressed part of disability planning. Led by Dr. Kiran Gupta, the workshop translated complex legal and financial concepts into practical steps for families.

Who took part

31 participants attended, representing a diverse mix:

  • 20Parents
  • 5Self-advocates
  • 4Board members
  • 2Office administrators

Key outcomes

  1. High engagement, high satisfaction

    Attendees said the workshop went beyond its stated scope, bringing clarity on broader life planning — not just documentation.

  2. Long-term commitment

    6 participants became new Lifetime Members of ASI.

  3. From knowledge to action

    Participants learned how to structure a Letter of Intent, set up a Private Trust for long-term care, and navigate legal, financial and caregiving continuity.

  4. Community building

    Meeting in person enabled peer learning and emotional reassurance. Parents and self-advocates shared lived experience and strengthened the support network.

Why in-person workshops still matter

Complex, high-stakes topics like disability planning benefit from meeting in person:

  • Real-time Q&A reduces confusion
  • Trust builds faster
  • Learning goes deeper and lasts longer

Looking ahead

Given the strong response, ASI hopes to run similar workshops in more cities — possibly in a hybrid format — to reach more families while keeping the depth of engagement.

Bottom line: this was not just a workshop — it was a catalyst for informed decisions, a stronger community, and long-term commitment to structured care planning.
