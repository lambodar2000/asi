The aim

What we exist to do.

The aim of Autism Society of India is to function as a national body for concerted action on all matters relating to persons with Autism Spectrum Disorders and their families.

Our objectives aim to improve the quality of life of individuals with ASD by securing for them all the fundamental rights enjoyed by every citizen under the Constitution of India.

This includes the provision of

  • Remedial
  • Educational
  • Training
  • Employment
  • Respite
  • Residential
  • Welfare
  • Social security services
  • Legal rights and protection

The Autism Society of India will

Five things we commit to.

Plus our promise: to be an independent and strong voice of the autism community across India.

  1. Representation

    Advocate to secure adequate representation in policymaking and implementing bodies of the Central and State Governments and local bodies.

  2. Legal rights

    Advocate for the legal rights and protection of persons with Autism Spectrum Disorders.

  3. Education & social rights

    Advocate for education (special and inclusive) together with the social rights of persons with Autism Spectrum Disorders.

  4. Common bond

    Form a common bond of understanding, cooperation and action among professionals, parents, families and organisations working in intellectual and developmental disabilities, nationally and internationally.

  5. Awareness & training

    Promote awareness and sensitisation in the community and in medical, legal, educational and related fields through awareness and training programmes.
